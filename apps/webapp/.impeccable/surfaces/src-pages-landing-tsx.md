# Public landing page

Mode: Persuade. Visitor: anyone wanting to unlock their voice. Action: Try a free review (record/upload). Proof: a labeled sample take, reviewed, shown as the product shows it. Companion route: /components.

Founder steer (2026-10-02): the platform should feel like a modern notebook with widgets, each lesson saved on one endless page, but the notebook is an undertone, not the whole personality. Keep Archivo, Martian Mono, Lucide icons, the five foundation colors and orange glass; bright, characterful, personal. Blend with the Night City company identity rather than replace it. Rhythm means visual rhythm: one spacing cadence, everything on the grid.

Open: review API hosting and abuse limits; naming Vinh (default: not named); logo placeholder.

Recorded adaptations (2026-10-02, after the finish review):
- Stage log: the nav lights the section in view rather than naming Learn/Record/Review/Retake, because the page does not run in loop order (it opens on a reviewed sample) and the promises band is not a stage; stage names on these anchors would mislabel them.
- Trend: take-to-take trend lives in the retake panel (labelled illustration). A credits/verdict table under the hero cover was removed at the founder's request (2026-10-02) because it pulled attention from listening; the hero is headline, one line, Try a free review + See an example, with the cover as the play object.

Founder copy rules (2026-10-02):
- Sell the outcome: unlock your voice, become confident, reach your potential, learn and get better. Covers are brand texture, never a feature in copy.
- The hero stays sparse: headline, one line, two buttons, cover, example. Anything that competes with listening to the example (credits, verdict tables, badges, explanatory captions) is out.
- The hero cover is promotional: all five foundation colours, no dashed outlines or empty ridges. Real take covers colour each line by its first strength and leave lines without one as a dashed outline, so covers fill up as you practise.
- Honesty about the example (synthetic voice, notes written by hand) lives in the footer and the "Illustration" label in the retake panel.

## Direction contract

THESIS: Each lesson unfolds like the liner booklet of an album from a Night City label: the take is the track, its transcript the lyric sheet, the review written in its margins. Refuses the coaching-app hero-screenshot-feature-grid and the literal bullet-journal template.
OWN-WORLD: Paper-white booklet panels on paper ground, ink type in wide Archivo, timecodes in Martian Mono. Graphite album covers drawn from the take itself: stacked cloud-soft ridges of each line's level, filled in the foundation of that line's note. Foundation fills as multiplied highlighter on words; pins filled/open, solid/dashed. One orange glass mark for whatever is live. Raised: every reading shows trend across takes (from six-pack); nothing off-grid, one 8px cadence (from arcade); the section in view lit in the nav with an orange tick, like a log of where you are (from raku).
STORY: The visitor plays the sample, watches the lyric sheet light word by word with notes in its margin, sees five lessons as a track list whose pages keep every take, sees take one and two of a line, reads what the coach never says, records their own.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Left five columns: the square graphite promotional cover (five ridges, one per foundation colour, from the example's real level). Right seven: the headline "Unlock the full potential of your voice.", one outcome line (learn, practise with a coach that listens, grow more confident one take at a time), Try a free review (primary) and See an example 0:15 (outline; scrolls to the notes and plays), then the lyric sheet. No captions, credits, badges or tables near the cover.
